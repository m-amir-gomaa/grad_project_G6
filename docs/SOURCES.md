# Official references and initial location labels

Sources inspected on **22 October 2026**:

- [Tanta FCI teaching schedules](https://ci.tanta.edu.eg/stu_study_tables.aspx)
- [Tanta FCI examination schedules](https://ci.tanta.edu.eg/stu-exam-tables.aspx)
- Representative PDFs are linked beside their layouts in [Timetable format](TIMETABLE-FORMAT.md).

The local scan covered **73 distinct timetable PDFs, 137 pages**, including **28 pages processed with Arabic/English OCR**. It found 648 location-label appearances grouped into the following 20 provisional labels. These are not counts of distinct physical rooms.

| Label group | Observed names |
|---|---|
| Central halls | المركزي العلوي، المركزي السفلي |
| Numbered lecture halls | مدرج 1، مدرج 2، مدرج 3 |
| Numbered laboratories | معمل 1، معمل 2 |
| Lettered laboratories | معمل C، معمل D، معمل E، معمل F، معمل G، معمل H |
| Bare location labels | C، D، E، F، G، H |
| Online delivery | أونلاين |

Confirm aliases, building/floor placement, capacities, and availability before using these labels as scheduling resources. For example, `C` and `معمل C` may refer to the same room; the PDFs alone do not establish that identity. The central-hall wording does not establish a numbered floor.

The current standalone product uses manually entered rooms/courses and student registrations entered individually or through the specified year CSV. The scan is historical research, not an automatic import or an official integration. Source documents supply naming and presentation examples; they are not a complete architectural survey or student registry.
